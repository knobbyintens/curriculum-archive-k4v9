# Algebra I — Course Syllabus

Course code: MATH-101
Credit hours: 3
Term: 2026 autumn

## Learning Outcomes
1. Manipulate algebraic expressions confidently.
2. Solve linear and quadratic equations.
3. Apply algebraic reasoning to word problems.

## Assessment
- Midterm exam: 30%
- Final exam: 45%
- Weekly problem sets: 25%

## Schedule
Two lectures and one tutorial per week.
